# Microwave interaction prototype

Open index.html in a modern browser. No installation or build step is required.

## Interaction flow

1. Open the door, place food inside, and close the door.
2. Choose Low, Medium, or High power and enter minutes and seconds.
3. Press Start to heat. The door locks and Open Door is disabled.
4. Press Stop once to pause and unlock the door.
5. Close the door and press Start to resume the remaining countdown.
6. Tap Stop twice within 400 ms to clear the timer to 00:00 and reset power to High.
7. At zero, heating stops, the door unlocks, a beep sounds when browser audio is available, and Heating complete is displayed.

Files: index.html (structure), style.css (appearance), and script.js (interaction logic). Keep all three in the same folder.
